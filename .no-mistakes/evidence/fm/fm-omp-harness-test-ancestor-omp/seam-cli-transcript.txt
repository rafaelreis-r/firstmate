### The FM_HARNESS_PS_BIN seam at the CLI, driven by hand

1) unset  -> the default 'ps'; a real omp parent is seen
   $ $NAMED/omp -c fm-harness.sh   ->  omp

2) empty  -> :- default keeps the real 'ps'; behaviour identical to (1)
   $ FM_HARNESS_PS_BIN= ... ->  omp

3) a ps that reports parent 0 at the caller -> the walk stops, no ancestry evidence
   $ FM_HARNESS_PS_BIN=$STOP/ps ... ->  

4) adversarial: the seam points at a path that does not exist
   $ FM_HARNESS_PS_BIN=/nonexistent/ps ... ->  detect=unknown exit=0
   (degrades to a marker-only verdict; no crash, no hang)
